# Diabetes Dataset - Data Dictionary

This document explains all features used in the diabetes prediction model.

---

## 1. HighBP (High Blood Pressure)
- 0 = No high blood pressure
- 1 = Has high blood pressure

---

## 2. HighChol (High Cholesterol)
- 0 = No high cholesterol
- 1 = Has high cholesterol

---

## 3. CholCheck (Cholesterol Check)
- 0 = No cholesterol check in last 5 years
- 1 = Had cholesterol check in last 5 years

---

## 4. BMI (Body Mass Index)
- Continuous numeric value
- Example: 18.5, 24.0, 30.2

---

## 5. Smoker
- 0 = Never smoked
- 1 = Smoked at least 100 cigarettes in lifetime

---

## 6. Stroke
- 0 = No stroke history
- 1 = Had stroke

---

## 7. HeartDiseaseorAttack
- 0 = No heart disease or heart attack
- 1 = Has heart disease or heart attack history

---

## 8. PhysActivity (Physical Activity)
- 0 = No physical activity
- 1 = Has physical activity

---

## 9. Fruits
- 0 = Does not consume fruits regularly
- 1 = Consumes fruits regularly

---

## 10. Veggies
- 0 = Does not consume vegetables regularly
- 1 = Consumes vegetables regularly

---

## 11. HvyAlcoholConsump (Heavy Alcohol Consumption)
- 0 = No heavy alcohol consumption
- 1 = Heavy alcohol consumption

---

## 12. AnyHealthcare
- 0 = No healthcare coverage
- 1 = Has healthcare coverage

---

## 13. NoDocbcCost (Doctor Visit Cost Issue)
- 0 = Could afford doctor visit
- 1 = Could not afford doctor visit due to cost

---

## 14. GenHlth (General Health)
- 1 = Excellent
- 2 = Very Good
- 3 = Good
- 4 = Fair
- 5 = Poor

---

## 15. MentHlth (Mental Health Days)
- Number of days mental health was not good (0–30)

---

## 16. PhysHlth (Physical Health Days)
- Number of days physical health was not good (0–30)

---

## 17. DiffWalk (Difficulty Walking)
- 0 = No difficulty walking
- 1 = Has difficulty walking

---

## 18. Sex
- 0 = Female
- 1 = Male

---

## 19. Age (Age Category)
- 1 = 18–24
- 2 = 25–29
- 3 = 30–34
- 4 = 35–39
- 5 = 40–44
- 6 = 45–49
- 7 = 50–54
- 8 = 55–59
- 9 = 60–64
- 10 = 65–69
- 11 = 70–74
- 12 = 75–79
- 13 = 80 or older

---

## 20. Education
- 1 = Never attended school / kindergarten only
- 2 = Elementary school
- 3 = Some high school
- 4 = High school graduate
- 5 = College or technical school
- 6 = College graduate

---

## 21. Income
- 1 = Less than $10,000
- 2 = $10,000–$15,000
- 3 = $15,000–$20,000
- 4 = $20,000–$25,000
- 5 = $25,000–$35,000
- 6 = $35,000–$50,000
- 7 = $50,000–$75,000
- 8 = $75,000 or more

---

# Notes
- This dataset is used for binary classification (Diabetes prediction)
- Target variable:
  - 0 = Non-Diabetic
  - 1 = Diabetic
